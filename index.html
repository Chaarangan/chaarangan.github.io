What I work on now

Most of my time goes to three problems. The first is search that respects each user's file permissions over hundreds of terabytes of customer storage on NetApp ONTAP, without copying the data into a vector index. The second is the agent runtime: sandboxes, memory that outlasts the context window, and policies an admin can attach to any agent or workflow. The third is shipping all of it to sites with no network at all, down to STIG-hardened, FIPS-enabled RHEL.

Case study: Generate Iterate.ai →

I've owned Generate's architecture and roadmap since it was an edge app on Intel AI PCs. It is now multi-tenant SaaS, on-prem, and air-gapped, from one codebase.

  • ConstraintSome customers can't send a single document out. Air-gapped installs had to keep every feature, including for a U.S. Federal Defense Agency.
  • DecisionSearch files where they live. Permission-aware search reads storage in place and checks each user's NFSv4 ACLs first. It answers over 400 TB+ at p95 under 1 second, with no embeddings and no content index. Patent pending.
  • DecisionIsolate every agent session. Each one runs in its own gVisor or Kata sandbox, so a compromised session can't reach another tenant. Versioned policies and DLP attach to any project, agent, or workflow.
  • DecisionInstall with one command, anywhere. I built Generate Sentinel, which installs the full stack online or air-gapped and publishes releases from GitHub Actions through OIDC roles into AWS.
  • Trade-offEvery model is certified before it serves. Each AI service binds to its own model, which costs a compatibility run per model and keeps vendor failures out of production.
  • Outcome99.8% uptime at 50,000+ requests/second. Consolidating services cut infrastructure cost 33% and made the platform 4× faster.
  • OutcomeIn production across industries. Healthcare claims and remittance through Cerner, launched with IBM and Hutchinson Regional Healthcare. Scheduled retail agents at Vuori. Storage editions with NetApp.
  • RecognitionPinnacle and TMC AI Product of the Year (2025). Iterate.ai made the CRN AI 100 in 2026.
