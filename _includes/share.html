{%- comment -%} Share links. url_encode, not uri_escape: uri_escape leaves ":" and "/" literal, which happens to work today but breaks the moment a shared URL carries its own query string. url_encode percent-encodes the whole value, which is what a query parameter actually requires. Each platform builds its preview by fetching the URL, so a post that is not yet deployed will share as a bare link with no card. LinkedIn in particular cannot be given prefilled text at all -- it renders whatever the page's Open Graph tags say, which _includes/head.html emits per post. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign share_url = page.url | absolute_url | url_encode -%} {%- assign share_title = page.title | url_encode -%}
